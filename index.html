Magic not Metric

Trading magic for metric.

Drones of nothingness. Breathing, but not living. The slow onset of numbness—until something in you remembers the sky.

Descend inward

We mistook the count for the life.

Somewhere the ledger became the world. Days stacked like empty rows. Motion without meaning. A pulse that keeps time and forgets why it began.

Metrics are fluent in volume and silent on wonder. They can name how much you did, never whether anything in you still shone. Keep trading long enough and the magic thins—then goes quiet.

This is not a call to abandon the work. It is a refusal to become the drone: present in body, absent in spirit, optimizing a life that no longer feels lived.

Signs of the slow numbness.

  1. 01

    The hollow harvest.

    You meet the target and feel nothing. Applause lands like dust. The chart climbs while the chest goes still—proof that the wrong thing was measured.

  2. 02

    Breathing, not living.

    Inhale, exhale, reply, repeat. The body keeps its rhythm while the day passes uninhabited—an automaton of competence, a ghost in its own calendar.

  3. 03

    Wonder sold cheap.

    Awe becomes inefficiency. Silence becomes lost time. What once startled you into aliveness is filed under distraction—and the cosmos is quietly swapped for a scoreboard.

  4. 04

    The drone’s comfort.

    Numbness arrives as ease: no risk of feeling too much, no demand to choose what matters. Nothingness has a soft voice. It asks only that you keep scrolling the metrics.

Yet the cosmos remains—within.

Reclaim the breath.

Three living breaths—not to optimize, but to remember you are more than the count.

Touch the light. Feel what the numbers cannot.

The sky was never outside you.

Let the drones hum. Let the ledgers fill. Somewhere beneath the numbness, a quieter vastness still waits—untallied, unbroken, yours. Magic not metric. The cosmos remains—within.
