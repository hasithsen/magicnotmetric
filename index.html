Magic not Metric

Remember you are human.

Enter the quiet

The measured life forgets the wind.

We trade wonder for proof. Hours become units. Breath becomes output. The body continues; the soul grows thin.

And yet—above the noise, the night is still wide. Sand still holds heat. A single star still asks nothing of you but to look.

Balance is not a score. It is the courage to remain alive inside your own life.

One breath under open sky.

No streak. No total. Only returning.

Press the light. Follow it home.

What you seek is already breathing with you.

Go gently. Leave something uncounted.
