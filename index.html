Ways back

Small acts of being alive.

  • Look up Leave the screen. Meet the sky, a tree, another face.
  • Protect unmeasured time Walk without a podcast. Cook without a timer. Let minutes be soft.
  • Feel before you track Ask how you are, not how you performed. Start there.
  • Keep a private wonder A sketch, a song, a silly ritual—something that refuses a KPI.
