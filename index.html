Magic not Metric

The same truths turn in every sky.

Listen again

What rises, returns.

Breath. Tide. Season. Starlight bent into dawn. The pattern is older than language, and it is still writing you—expand, hold, release, begin.

The age of counting forgets this. It straightens the circle into a line, then wonders why the heart grows tired of running nowhere.

Laws written in light.

  1. 01

    As above, so within.

    The night you walk under is the night you carry.

  2. 02

    What is essential is invisible.

    Love, loyalty, wonder—felt before they can be proven.

  3. 03

    Attention is creation.

    What you stay with becomes yours; what you rush past thins away.

  4. 04

    Nothing true is only once.

    Every ending is a hinge. Every silence, a seed.

Balance is remembering the circle while the world sells you the line.

Practice the oldest motion.

Three turns of the same wheel. No score. Only return.

Touch the light. Enter the cycle.

You are the pattern, briefly awake.

Not a sum. A turn of the same eternal wheel—sand, wind, star, and the quiet decision to remain human.
