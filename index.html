Magic not Metric

See with the heart.

Begin the descent

The world will ask you to count.

How many hours. How much progress. How far ahead. It will call this seriousness—and leave you strangely empty, as if you had flown all night and forgotten the stars.

Grown minds love numbers. Living ones love what numbers cannot hold: a sudden laugh, a loyal silence, the gold edge of evening on the sand.

What is essential is invisible.

Beneath the dry expanse, a well still waits. Above it, one star may become yours—not because you measured it, but because you stayed long enough to care.

Balance is that staying. Not a score. A vow to remain human.

Sit with one star.

Three breaths. No ledger. Only light.

Touch the light when you are ready.

You are not a sum.

You are a traveler beneath an endless roof of quiet fire. Go gently—and leave something wild uncounted.
